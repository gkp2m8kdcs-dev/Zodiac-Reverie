Frequently asked

Does Zodiac Reverie work offline?

Yes. Every reading, game, and Library item is generated and stored locally on your device. The app does not need an internet connection to function.

Does the app collect my data?

No. There is no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising SDK, no tracking. Your selected zodiac sign, your daily reading, your saved Library items and any photo you use in Photo Reading stay on your phone. The full Privacy Policy is available here.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Photo Reading uses the front camera or your photo library only on that screen, and the selected image stays in the app's private container. It is not transmitted, scanned, or backed up by the app. You can delete it at any time from the Library, or clear all app data from iOS Settings.

My daily reading hasn't refreshed — what should I do?

Readings refresh at midnight in your device's local time. If the date has changed but the reading has not, open the app once on the home tab — it will refresh as soon as you land on the screen. Force-quitting and reopening the app is rarely needed.

How do I delete everything?

Open Settings → Clear All inside the app to wipe your Library, photos, and reading cache. Alternatively, go to iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Zodiac Reverie → Delete App. This removes every byte of app data from your device.

I don't know my Chinese zodiac sign — how do I find it?

On the home screen of the app, tap your current sign or the picker at the top. Zodiac Reverie offers a year-by-year guide for the twelve animals, so you can confirm your sign by year.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Send a note to Mefepzavap@icloud.com. Please include your iOS version, your iPhone model, and a short description. Screenshots are always welcome.

Is there an Android version?

At this time, Zodiac Reverie is iOS only.

Is the reading for entertainment only?

Yes. Zodiac Reverie is intended for entertainment and personal reflection only. It is not a substitute for professional advice of any kind — medical, legal, financial, psychological, or otherwise. Please do not make significant life decisions based on the app's output.
